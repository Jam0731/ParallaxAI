An example, worked through

Give the symbols
something to mean.

A fixed charge and a cost per item can become an algebraic expression. Here is a short exercise, with the steps shown.

This is an illustration of the teaching approach, not a student result or testimonial.

Example exerciseEveryday costs → Algebra

“A workshop charges £18 plus £6 per item. Write an expression for the cost of n items, then calculate the cost for four items.”

£18Fixed charge
+
£6 × nCost per item × Items

Cost in pounds: 18 + 6n

For four items: 18 + (6 × 4) = £42

n stands for the number of items. The £18 stays the same; the item cost changes with n.

Practical questions

Before you get in touch.

Is this for someone who has been out of education?

Yes. Lumen Hours is for adults returning to mathematics: refreshing algebra, preparing for a technical course or rebuilding confidence with everyday maths.

What happens in the free fit call?

It is a free 15-minute conversation to discuss fit. It does not include a lesson. Email Sam to arrange it.

How do I choose what to study?

You choose a relevant topic together with Sam. You can describe the maths you want help with in your first email.

How long is a lesson, and what does it cost?

Each one-to-one online lesson is 50 minutes and costs GBP 45, paid per session. There is no subscription or package discount.

Will I have anything to work on afterwards?

Sam provides a short practice sheet after a lesson. A recorded course is not part of this service.

When can I book, and which online platform is used?

Arrange the fit call by email and ask Sam about current availability and the online meeting details. The page does not offer live booking.

What if I need to cancel or rearrange?

Ask Sam for the cancellation and rearrangement terms before agreeing to a lesson.

Do you teach children or guarantee grades?

No. This service is for adults only. Tuition does not guarantee exam results, admission or any particular grade.
