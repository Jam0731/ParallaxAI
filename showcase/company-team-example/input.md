## Product requirements

- A complete locally usable web application with responsive styling and clear instructions. It must run through a normal static HTTP preview; no backend or external resource is needed.
- Editable monthly total working hours, non-client work hours and reserved buffer hours. Available client capacity is total hours minus non-client work and buffer; do not quietly make impossible negative capacity look valid.
- Add, edit and remove named client rows, each with committed monthly hours and a monthly fee in one chosen currency. Preserve names as literal text. Avoid labeling revenue as profit.
- Show available client capacity, total committed client hours, remaining capacity or the overbooked amount, total monthly fee, and effective revenue per committed client hour. When committed hours are zero, explain that the effective hourly figure is not defined.
- Clear validation for blank, negative, nonnumeric and inconsistent inputs; never silently turn missing values into an apparently valid zero.
- Save and restore the user's scenario locally, with a clear reset control. Provide an export useful to the owner, such as a readable CSV or JSON file. Tell the owner where data is stored and what reset removes.
