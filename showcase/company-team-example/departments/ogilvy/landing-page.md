# See whether your next retainer fits.

Ledgerleaf is a monthly capacity planner for independent designers. Put your available client hours and existing retainers on one screen, then add a prospect to see how the month changes.

**Try the local prototype with fictional clients.** Reply **“planner”** to the post or message that brought you here to request an opt-in test.

## Give the month a realistic limit

Start with your total working hours. Set aside time for non-client work and a buffer. Ledgerleaf shows the hours left for client commitments.

Add each retainer's name, monthly hours and fee in USD. Edit or remove a row as your plans change. The totals show:

- Available client capacity and hours already committed.
- Hours remaining, or how far the plan is overbooked.
- Total monthly client fees and revenue per committed client hour.

If required values are missing or invalid, the planner asks you to correct them. Non-client work and buffer cannot exceed the month's total hours. When there are no committed client hours, the hourly revenue figure is undefined.

## Try this fictional month

| Monthly plan | Hours |
| --- | ---: |
| Total working time | 120 |
| Non-client work | 24 |
| Reserved buffer | 12 |
| Available for clients | 84 |
| Existing retainers | 78 |
| Remaining capacity | 6 |

The starter's three fictional clients total USD 5,220 in monthly fees. Add a prospect needing 12 hours at USD 900 a month yourself. Committed time becomes 90 hours, putting the plan **6 hours over capacity**. Monthly fees become USD 6,120.

The fee totals describe the scenario you entered. They are revenue figures before expenses and taxes, not profit or a prediction of what you will earn.

## Keep a local scenario

Use the labeled fictional starter or begin with blank inputs. The prototype runs in a browser through a local static preview, without an account or backend. The app makes no external calls.

Your scenario saves in this browser profile for the preview address. It does not sync to another device or browser. Clearing browser storage, using a temporary private session or changing the preview address can make that saved scenario unavailable.

Export a readable JSON snapshot when you want a separate copy. Export is not an automatic backup or a promise of import support. Reset clears this app's saved scenario and returns the planner to blank inputs. It does not delete exported files.

## One designer, one month

Ledgerleaf covers monthly retainer hours and fees in USD. It does not include payroll, invoices, timesheets, team permissions or calendar scheduling. Your estimates still determine whether the plan is useful.

A spreadsheet can already do this arithmetic. The question for this prototype is whether a dedicated screen makes this particular decision more convenient for you.

## Help test that question

We are testing a possible **USD 9 one-time price**. That is a pricing hypothesis. The current local prototype test is free, and there is no checkout or purchase available here.

If you are an independent designer with at least one retainer, reply **“planner”** where this page was shared. You can take part in a short, opt-in test using the fictional month, compare it with your current method and tell us what you would keep or change. No private client information is needed.
