## Post 1 — The capacity calculation

Source ID: POST-01. Intended context: professional feed or a permitted independent-design community thread.

---

A 12-hour retainer can be too much for a 120-hour month.

Here is a fictional example: reserve 24 hours for non-client work and 12 for a buffer. That leaves 84 client hours. If existing retainers use 78, there are only 6 left. Adding the new 12-hour client puts the plan 6 hours over capacity.

Ledgerleaf puts that monthly calculation on one screen for independent designers. Enter your hours, add or edit retainers, and see remaining capacity alongside monthly fees in USD.

It is a local browser prototype with a fictional starter, local saving and JSON export. No account is needed. A possible USD 9 one-time price is still a hypothesis; this test is free.

If you manage at least one retainer and want to try the fictional month, reply **“planner.”** We can arrange a short opt-in test. Please keep real client details out of the comments.
